# Octave Eight Fulfillment — Project Context

## Working business name
**Octave Eight Fulfillment** (working name only; do not imply trademark/business-name clearance).

The name references an octave in music and the eight founders. Keep the identity tasteful rather than gimmicky: rhythm, coordination, precision, eight people working as one.

## Current business state
- Pre-launch regional 3PL / ecommerce fulfillment company focused on Northern California.
- Eight founders.
- Initial target: smaller ecommerce / Shopify brands, initially around low-to-mid hundreds of monthly orders, with room to scale into larger clients.
- Initial product scope: ordinary non-regulated, non-hazardous physical consumer goods. Avoid making claims about food, supplements, pharmaceuticals, alcohol, cannabis, hazardous materials, refrigerated goods, or other regulated categories.
- Core launch service concept: receiving, storage, inventory handling, pick/pack/ship, carrier handoff, returns, and responsive client support.
- Shopify/WMS integration is central to the operating model. The founders are still evaluating WMS options; do not state that a specific WMS is final.
- Same-day fulfillment is the target for eligible orders received before 3 PM, subject to operational capacity, service agreement, and carrier cutoff times. Do not write this as an unconditional legal guarantee.
- Customer-service differentiators discussed by founders: direct human communication, quick response, regional accessibility, white-glove attention, and avoiding the feeling of being a small client in a giant 3PL.
- Pricing is still being finalized. Current simple draft volume structure:
  - Starter: 100–499 orders/mo — $3.50/order including first unit
  - Growth: 500–1,499 — $3.25/order including first unit
  - Scale: 1,500–4,999 — $3.00/order including first unit
  - Enterprise: 5,000+ — custom quote
  - Additional units: currently proposed at $0.60/unit
- Other current proposed rates (editable, not final): receiving $45/hr; pallet storage $30/mo; shelf $20/mo; bin $8/mo; basic returns $5; simple kitting $2/completed kit; special projects $55/hr + materials; packaging and postage separate.
- Do not make claims such as 24/7 support, nationwide fulfillment network, guaranteed carrier delivery times, proprietary technology, existing customers, years in business, or certifications that do not exist yet.

## Website goal
The website is primarily a credible sales/lead-generation site. It should make a growing ecommerce owner think: "These people are responsive, transparent, regional, and I can actually talk to them."

Primary CTA: request a fulfillment review / talk to the founding team.
Secondary CTA: view services / see simple pricing.

## Tone
- Professional, modern, clear, grounded.
- Boutique service, not flashy startup hype.
- Avoid AI buzzwords.
- Avoid claiming to be the cheapest.
- Avoid overusing the eight/octave concept; use it subtly in the brand story and visual rhythm.
